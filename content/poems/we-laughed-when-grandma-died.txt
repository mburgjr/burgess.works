Plastic chairs by the pool cracking
Under bellies of beer
Concealing the things which sit within us

They come out through fragmented sentences
Or broken promises
On late nights we let the potion probe truth through tangents of verbose ramblings, scrambling to get someone who can't hear us to understand

It’s rather bland to say what you are plainly thinking
But it must be necessary cause it happens eventually

Laughter is the only medicine
Laughter takes the truth and pushes it forward under a bedazzled cloak
The recipient may choose to disrobe the joke but that would break the oath
And it is much easier to cackle than choke

We know how to tell a story
Strung from held memories of things that secretly matter
This way we can release all the boring gore
With a delivery that is shiny, yet scattered

So yeah we laughed when grandma died
We laughed about how she couldn't stick out her tongue
We laughed about how she gave out kit-kats, not hugs
We laughed about the time she tripped over her oxygen tank
We laughed about the time she drank and drank and drank
