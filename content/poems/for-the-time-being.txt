We sit in crowded halls with hampers
Twiddling our thumbs while dirt collects.
We jump over leaves and branches
To hurdle the horror of mounting regret.

It's clear to me to see that we
Are hardly moving forward.
But, not moving at all
Would be worse than torture.

I mean...

You can't buy love at the candy store,
But you can drink all the liquor you may.

You can't buy hope at the candle store,
But you can flicker the flame.

You can't stay silent in every room,
Cause sometimes you can't stand the pain.

You can't have it all so very soon,
But you can keep chipping away.
