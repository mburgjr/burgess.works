Love occurs quietly
By a lampshade
In the barefooted comfort
Of a carpeted home

Love is shockingly gentle
And awkwardly sprung
Like a calm in the breeze
Like a little baby duck

Love is shared
Without cause for dismay
Like a pizza
Or a story from your day

Love is a discussion
A negotiation of joy
"No, this first", "no, that first"
"We can have 'em both?" "Are you sure?"

Love is repetitive
Cyclic and mundane
It's my nightly routine
It's the reason I wake

Love is what my mother gifted me
On the first day of my birth
Love is what will be lept from me
When she someday crawls back into the dirt
