Usher of the summer
Enemy of bummer
Soar past tundra
Above
My envious grin

Moon kisser
Truth giver
Yet always a bit dimmer
When trouble begins

Bright shiner
Dark finder
Provide your spare light
To my frozen shadows

Plentiful scrooge
Hoarder of hue
Am I no longer due,
For some heat on my back?

Gracious withholder!
Timely kind soldier!
Blessing my cold soul
With warm gifts that you bring

Heavenly grocer!
Cautious approacher!
Teach me how you do it
Over
And over again
