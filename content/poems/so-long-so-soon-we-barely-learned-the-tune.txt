Ahoy, young lad
Have you gathered the news from the latest weather report?

The great leviathan has been sent extinct with last dusk
The emperor of the seas who shaped the tide over centuries thus
This tremendous whale, my friend, that stretched for meters and transcended circumferences has ceased and sunk with its wisdom in tow
The gentle titan that berthed the ages has resigned to hibernate into depths of its own

Rush not to pray
Rush not in any endeavor
For it is this breakneck haste which snapped our heroes' humped-back

You see, my young boy
The deities of our sea transmit their will through frequencies at such slow speeds that we'd not hear them with a seismic telescope
Their voice, it cascades across continents with the same tenderness from which it is formed
Intonating their reverent benevolence, letting it resonate for eons beyond

Alas, child
We became too greedy and impatient
We bastardized the wishes of our sweet fishes
They tried to steer the vessel, but we veered and missed their sought mark

And so long to
Our silent makers of beauty
They could no longer sing their compositions at the heightened tempo of the new millennia
Even John Coltrane's saxophone could not keep pace
They could no longer architect the natural world under tightened deadlines of corporate America
Even Frank Lloyd Wright would ask for an extra day

Oh, heavens below!
I -- beg -- you -- to -- abandon -- us -- not
For it is only the richness of your oily blubber that can ignite our callused hearts
And bring light to burn away our malicious ignorant thoughts
