Phrases sit on a mountaintop
Guarded by the empty mind.
The fence runs from north to south
As tall as the world is vague.

The mischievous ruler sits as people knock,
His fingers like a hurried spider.
And laughs to the commune,
“How marvelous the deed stays done!”

The gateway’s in the valley lockbox,
To which no key is held.
Forever is played on a siren of silence,
The only thing an outsider can know.
